## 🤔 What is this?

This repo contains **reforged** versions of a few extensions — meaning I took
something that mostly worked, hit it with a hammer until it worked the way
*I* wanted, and then bolted it back together. Think less "fork with a
roadmap" and more "raccoon fixing a toaster."

**These are for personal use. Mine. Not yours. Well — technically now yours too, since it's public, but you know what I mean.**

If it breaks your browser, your workflow, your printer, or your relationship

## 📦 Installation

1. Clone this repo
2. Load it as an unpacked extension
3. Whisper "please work" to your screen
4. That's it, that's the whole guide
